# Smoke and Blackmail
*São Paulo, 1947 · seed 7*

**Victim:** Fausto Falcão, cabaret singer.
**Crime scene:** the body was found in the attic, at a run-down hotel in Santa Ifigênia.
**Weapon:** a bronze candlestick.

## Suspects
- **Januário Camargo** (brother): motive, an old grudge; says they were waiting for a train at Luz Station.
- **Aurélio Guedes** (neighbor): motive, an inheritance about to be rewritten; insists they were closing the register at a bakery.
- **Euclides Albuquerque** (godson): motive, blackmail that threatened to ruin their reputation; claims to have been playing cards in the back of a bar.
- **Berenice Barcellos** (business partner): motive, pathological jealousy; says they went to the movies on Avenida São João.

## Evidence
1. Only Januário Camargo had the key to the attic padlock.
   - ✅ Genuine clue, points to Januário Camargo.
2. Aurélio Guedes left town the next day.
   - ❌ Red herring: Aurélio Guedes was secretly negotiating the sale of their own business, which is why they acted suspiciously.
3. A stash of cash was found hidden in Euclides Albuquerque's home.
   - ❌ Red herring: Euclides Albuquerque was keeping a secret affair, which is why they acted suspiciously.
4. Witnesses saw Berenice Barcellos arguing with the victim hours before the crime.
   - ❌ Red herring: Berenice Barcellos frequented an illegal gambling den, which is why they acted suspiciously.
5. The bronze candlestick missing from the parlor turned up, hastily washed, at Januário Camargo's house.
   - ✅ Genuine clue, points to Januário Camargo.
6. Nobody heard Januário Camargo ask how the victim died.
   - ✅ Genuine clue, points to Januário Camargo.

## Solution
**Culprit:** Januário Camargo (brother).
**Motive:** an old grudge.
**Broken alibi:** Januário Camargo says they were waiting for a train at Luz Station, but the story does not hold up.
**Twist:** The clock at the scene was set forward to fake the time of death.
